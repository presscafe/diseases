A structured reference of livestock disease.

Every record answers the same four questions, in the same order. The point is not to collect descriptions but to force the causal chain to be explicit — from the agent, through what makes disease possible, to the mechanism that produces the signs, to what can actually be done about it.

Agents

What the cause is — pathogen, toxin, mineral, management factor or gene. Named precisely, with toxins, vectors and reservoirs where they apply.

Aetiology

What has to be true for disease to occur. Determinants and risk factors, not just a list of causes.

Pathophysiology

The ordered mechanism from exposure to lesion, and the clinical signs that mechanism explains.

Control & management

Prevention, vaccination, treatment, outbreak response and public health — separated, because they are different jobs.
